## Material corrections discovered

- EuroHPC SPT, QTI and QEXP topic-detail pages omit the participating-state restriction present in Amendment 5. The PDF prevents a false Korean eligibility approval. All six August quantum calls were excluded on that restriction.
- EuroHPC QML has an explicit 50% EU funding ceiling, not the default RIA 100%. Its general associated-country wording and country-list footnote cannot be reconciled confidently for Korea, so no page was published. No call-owner message was sent.
- IHI API repeats the EUR 53.2M call-wide budget per topic. Official topic allocations are EUR 35M, 9M and 9.2M. Topic 1 exceptionally lowers the private-contribution threshold to 30%; topics 2/3 retain 45%.
- RAISE 2027 topic 04 has an empty API budget-year map; Work Programme Part 14 p.8 supplies EUR 1M, bringing the call to EUR 30.5M.
- MISS 2027 Cancer topic 04 carries an internally inconsistent EUR 10M allocation, EUR 5M–7M expected contribution and 3 expected grants in both official sources. Published the stated allocation and disclosed the inconsistency; did not manufacture a derived per-project amount.
- NEB 2027-01 has 10 active topics plus a separate -CANCELLED record. The active REGEN-03 topic is valid; the cancelled record contributes no topic or budget to the published page.
- PCP calls fund buyers, while development companies may compete in downstream tenders. Cascade-support calls select operators first; future third-party maximum amounts are not immediate startup application grants.
